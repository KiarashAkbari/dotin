Verdict
- The sandbox did NOT reproduce the breach. Its post-load window was quiet: in both valid 'old' trials
  autovacuum had not started on the frame's relations by the time the step ended, and in the alternating
  trials the best repeat was 360.6-370.5 ms with or without the settle (363.2-380.3 ms in the three full
  chains with the settle).
- What the settle demonstrably does here: it costs 0.79-0.84 s for 13 relations, it leaves every relation
  the frame depends on vacuumed and analysed before the first repeat, and autovacuum then has no pending
  work on them (0 autovacuum/autoanalyze passes on the 13 after the full chain).
- Whether autovacuum, unset hint bits / visibility map, or something outside the relations (a checkpoint
  or OS writeback of the bulk load) slowed the student's run is not established. The settle removes the
  first two; the third is not covered. The new refusal message prints every run and the autovacuum
  samples, so the next run on that machine says which case it was.
