What two `200` lines are worth here, stated narrowly. They are the two URLs the shipped code actually
calls — the aggregate body and the administrative municipality list — from a network where the item's
question is a live one. A `200` on those two is the honest form of «reachable and no key asked», and it is
the third such datum: `patch-0017-student-run.txt` already records `fetched: 5570 row(s)` /
`stored: 5570 row(s)` taken from his machine against `olist_phase0_test`, which is a bulk fetch of the same
shape rather than a single probe.

What is deliberately not claimed. No rate limit was studied, no quota, no behaviour under a burst of
requests; the project's own budget (at most one extra reference request per run, and only when the signal
response omits the state) is a constraint we imposed, not evidence that the host would have refused more.
One request from his network and one bulk fetch do not describe a service's policy, and this file does not
pretend to.

The alternative is recorded, because the item asks for it in the same breath. `apisidra.ibge.gov.br/values/t/4714/n6/all/v/93/p/2022`
is the SIDRA mirror named in `decisions.md` §۸ as the escape hatch; switching to it is a new `CONTRACTS`
entry plus a config URL, not a rewrite, and that is the whole of what «یا جایگزینش ثبت شود» requires. The
mirror answers with a different shape (`resultados[].series[]` at the top level, per
`docs/evidence/ibge-4714-614-sample.json`), which is exactly why the parser reads paths instead of assuming
one key — see `decisions.md` §۱۲.
